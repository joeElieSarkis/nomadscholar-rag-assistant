CONTENT:
University scholarship and financial aid pages explain funding opportunities offered directly by universities.

Scholarship rules differ widely between universities. Some scholarships are merit-based, some are need-based, and some are limited to specific countries, programs, degree levels, or applicant profiles.

Students should check each university's official scholarship page for eligibility requirements, deadlines, application forms, required documents, renewal conditions, and whether a separate scholarship application is needed.

Common required documents may include academic transcripts, admission application, essays, recommendation letters, proof of financial need, passport information, standardized test results if required, and language proficiency results.

Students should not assume that admission automatically includes scholarship consideration. Some universities require separate financial aid forms or scholarship applications before a specific deadline.
