TITLE: DAAD Admission Requirements for Germany
SOURCE: DAAD official guidance on admission requirements
URL: https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/requirements/overview/

CONTENT:
Students who want to study in Germany usually need a recognized higher education entrance qualification for undergraduate study or a relevant previous degree for graduate study.

Admission requirements depend on the university, degree level, program, and applicant's country of education. Students should check whether their school certificate or university degree is recognized for admission to German higher education.

Applicants may need to provide academic transcripts, certificates, certified translations, proof of language proficiency, a curriculum vitae, a motivation letter, and other program-specific documents.

Some programs require German language proficiency, while others are taught in English and may require English language proof. Language requirements must be checked on the official program page.

Students should verify requirements through the university website, DAAD resources, and official admissions portals because requirements and deadlines differ between programs.
