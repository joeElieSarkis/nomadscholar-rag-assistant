TITLE: Common App International Students
SOURCE: Common App official international student guidance
URL: https://www.commonapp.org/apply/first-year-students

CONTENT:
Common App allows students to apply to many colleges and universities through one application platform.

International students may need to provide academic transcripts, school reports, recommendation letters, personal essays, English language proficiency results, standardized test scores if required, and financial documentation depending on the college.

Requirements vary by university. Students should always review the requirements for each college inside Common App and on the college's official admissions website.

International applicants should pay close attention to deadlines, application fees, essay prompts, testing policies, financial aid forms, and document submission instructions.

A strong application usually requires planning ahead, preparing documents early, checking college-specific requirements, and asking counselors or recommenders for support before the deadline.
