TITLE: Campus France Programs and Scholarships
SOURCE: Campus France official guidance for finding programs and scholarships in France
URL: https://www.campusfrance.org/en/finding-a-university-programme-France

CONTENT:
Campus France provides official guidance for international students who want to find university programs in France.

Students interested in a Master's program in Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Computer Science, Machine Learning, or related fields should use official program search tools rather than relying only on general advice.

Campus France provides a catalog of programs taught in English. This catalog can help international students find programs that are fully or partially taught in English at French higher education institutions. Students can search by field of study, degree level, institution, and location.

For a student interested in a Master's in AI in France, useful search keywords may include artificial intelligence, data science, computer science, machine learning, intelligent systems, robotics, software engineering, cybersecurity, and applied mathematics.

Students should check each program page carefully because admission requirements differ between universities and schools. A Master's application may require academic transcripts, a bachelor's degree or equivalent, CV, motivation letter, recommendation letters, proof of English or French language proficiency, passport information, and sometimes a portfolio, interview, or technical background in mathematics and programming.

Campus France also provides scholarship information for foreign students. Scholarship opportunities may depend on nationality, degree level, field of study, host institution, funding authority, duration, and academic profile. CampusBourses is a scholarship search tool that students can use to look for funding opportunities in France.

Students should verify final details directly from the official university program page, Campus France, the scholarship provider, or the Études en France platform when applicable. Requirements, deadlines, tuition fees, scholarships, and language conditions can change by program and academic year.
