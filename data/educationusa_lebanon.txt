TITLE: EducationUSA Lebanon Advising
SOURCE: AMIDEAST Lebanon / EducationUSA advising information
URL: https://www.amideast.org/lebanon/study-in-the-usa/study-in-the-usa

CONTENT:
EducationUSA Lebanon provides advising services for students in Lebanon who are interested in studying in the United States.

Advising can help students understand the U.S. higher education system, search for suitable universities, prepare application materials, understand standardized testing, explore financial aid options, and plan for the student visa process.

Students should use advising support as guidance, but they remain responsible for checking each university's official admissions requirements, deadlines, scholarship instructions, and required documents.

EducationUSA advising is especially useful for students who need help organizing the application timeline, comparing university options, and understanding how to finance their studies.
