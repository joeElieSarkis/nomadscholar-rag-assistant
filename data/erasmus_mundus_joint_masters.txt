TITLE: Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters
SOURCE: Erasmus Mundus official student guidance
URL: https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/opportunities-for-individuals/students/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters

CONTENT:
Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters are high-level international master's programs delivered by a consortium of universities in different countries.

Students apply directly to the selected Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters program. Each program has its own admission requirements, deadlines, selection criteria, required documents, and scholarship information.

Applicants may need to prepare academic transcripts, degree certificates, a curriculum vitae, motivation letters, recommendation letters, proof of language proficiency, passport information, and other program-specific documents.

Erasmus Mundus scholarships may cover participation costs, travel, visa-related costs, and living allowance, depending on the program and scholarship rules.

Students should check the official website of the specific master's program they are applying to because requirements, deadlines, and scholarship availability differ between programs.
