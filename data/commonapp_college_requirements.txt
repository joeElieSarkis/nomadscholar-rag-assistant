TITLE: Common App College Requirements
SOURCE: Common App college-specific requirements guidance
URL: https://www.commonapp.org/

CONTENT:
Common App colleges may have different application requirements even when students apply through the same platform.

Students should review each college's specific requirements before submitting an application. A college may require supplemental essays, recommendation letters, school reports, transcripts, test scores if applicable, portfolio materials, writing supplements, or additional questions.

Some colleges may be test-optional, while others may require or recommend standardized test scores. Testing policies can change, so applicants should verify the policy on the college's official admissions website.

Application deadlines also differ by college and admission plan. Students should check whether the college offers early decision, early action, regular decision, or rolling admission.

International students should also check whether the college requires English language proficiency results, financial documentation, translated transcripts, or credential evaluation.

The safest approach is to make a separate checklist for each college because requirements, deadlines, and scholarship instructions are not identical.
