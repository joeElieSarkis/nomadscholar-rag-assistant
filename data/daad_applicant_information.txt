TITLE: DAAD Applicant Information
SOURCE: DAAD official applicant information and scholarship database guidance
URL: https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/scholarships/daad-scholarships/

CONTENT:
DAAD scholarship applicants should read the official scholarship announcement carefully before applying.

Each DAAD scholarship program has its own eligibility criteria, academic level, target group, application deadline, language requirements, funding period, and required documents.

Applicants may need to submit a curriculum vitae, academic certificates, transcripts, motivation letter, recommendation letters, proof of language proficiency, admission documents, research proposal, study plan, portfolio, or work samples depending on the program.

Some documents may need certified translation or official formatting. Applicants should prepare early and confirm whether documents must be uploaded through the DAAD portal or sent through another official application system.

DAAD applicants should not assume that one general document list applies to all scholarships. The exact requirements must be checked in the selected scholarship program's official announcement.
