TITLE: Common App First-Year Application Guide
SOURCE: Common App official first-year application guidance
URL: https://www.commonapp.org/apply/first-year-students

CONTENT:
The Common App first-year application is used by students applying to undergraduate programs for the first time.

A first-year application commonly includes personal information, family information, education history, activities, writing sections, college-specific questions, school forms, recommendations, and application fees or fee waiver requests.

Students should carefully complete all required sections and review each college's individual requirements. Some colleges may require supplemental essays, portfolios, test scores, writing samples, or additional forms.

Deadlines vary by college and admission plan. Students should check whether they are applying through early action, early decision, regular decision, or rolling admission.

Applicants should give recommenders and school counselors enough time to submit required forms before the deadline.
